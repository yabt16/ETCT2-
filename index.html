About Us

Ethiopian Cadet Association is a maritime community created to support Ethiopian cadets before training, during onboard training, and after graduation.

Pre-Training

Guidance for new cadets, interview preparation, maritime basics, and school support.

Onboard Training

Technical support, safety awareness, onboard knowledge, and cadet networking.

Post-Training

Career opportunities, certification support, and maritime employment guidance.

Training Programs

Basic Safety Training

STCW courses and maritime safety preparation.

Engine Cadet Training

Engine room familiarization and technical preparation.

Deck Cadet Training

Navigation, bridge watchkeeping, and cargo operations.

Courses & Certifications

  • PST - Personal Survival Techniques
  • FFFP - Fire Prevention & Fire Fighting
  • PSSR - Personal Safety & Social Responsibility
  • EFA - Elementary First Aid
  • Oil Tanker Familiarization

Financial Support

We provide guidance for scholarships, sponsorships, cadet financial planning, and training support opportunities.
